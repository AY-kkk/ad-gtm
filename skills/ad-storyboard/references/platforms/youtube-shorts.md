# YouTube Shorts vertical brief

## Use when

The asset needs a compact result-first story that can be discovered, replayed, or connected to a longer product explanation.

## Planning fields

- Placement and current official duration / export limits: **fill and verify before delivery**
- Aspect ratio and safe area: **fill from current placement specification**
- Audience intent or search context:
- Result or promise visible at the start:
- Product proof and supporting link:
- Captions and voiceover:
- Loop or final-frame behavior:
- CTA and destination:
- `source_url` / `checked_at`:

## Creative route

1. Show the result or question immediately, then explain the smallest useful mechanism.
2. Use a clean final frame that can also function as the start of a replay.
3. Keep title, caption, and spoken language consistent; do not rely on a title to rescue an unclear video.
4. Provide a cutdown and a longer explanation when the product needs more proof.

## Preflight

Check current Shorts export and ad requirements, caption readability, music and talent rights, landing-page continuity, and loop behavior.
