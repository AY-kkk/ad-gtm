# TikTok vertical feed brief

## Use when

The ad should feel native to a creator-led feed and must work for both sound-on and sound-off viewing.

## Planning fields

- Placement and current official duration / export limits: **fill and verify before delivery**
- Aspect ratio and safe area: **fill from current placement specification**
- Objective and conversion event:
- Hook that works without context:
- Creator, actor, or brand voice and rights:
- On-screen captions and localization:
- Cleared audio or original sound:
- CTA and destination:
- `source_url` / `checked_at`:

## Creative route

1. Make the first frame understandable without the caption or profile context.
2. Use one creator-native behavior—selfie, reply, unboxing, or quick demo—as the delivery device.
3. Keep the product proposition concrete; a trend is an attention device, not evidence.
4. Deliver an evergreen fallback cut when the concept depends on a trend or audio track.

## Preflight

Check creator consent, paid-partnership disclosure, audio rights, caption safe area, current ad policies, and audio-off comprehension.
