# Instagram Reels vertical brief

## Use when

The ad relies on visual identity, creator collaboration, product styling, or a story that may be reused in Feed and Story placements.

## Planning fields

- Placement: Reels / Feed / Story variant
- Current official duration / export limits: **fill and verify before delivery**
- Aspect ratio and safe area: **fill from the selected placement specification**
- Visual identity and product fact:
- Creator or talent rights:
- Caption, stickers, and text layers:
- Audio rights and sound-off fallback:
- CTA and destination:
- `source_url` / `checked_at`:

## Creative route

1. Lead with a strong visual or human action that survives Feed and Reels cropping.
2. Keep text away from interface overlays and create a crop-safe master.
3. Use product detail and a clear next step before decorative brand transitions.
4. If a creator is involved, separate the creator's voice from any unverified testimonial.

## Preflight

Check each selected placement separately, current safe areas, branded-content disclosure, music and talent rights, caption legibility, and destination behavior.
