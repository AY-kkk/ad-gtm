# Douyin vertical feed brief

## Use when

The ad is intended for a fast-scrolling Chinese short-video feed, especially a product demonstration, direct-response offer, or live-commerce cutdown.

- `source_url` / `checked_at`:

## Creative route

1. Start with a recognizable problem, result, or physical action; do not spend the opening on a logo card.
2. Show the product action and its evidence before adding secondary features.
3. Keep the end card long enough for the CTA and destination to be read on a small screen.
4. Prepare a version without platform-native comments or trend audio so the asset has a controlled fallback.

## Preflight

Check current safe areas, subtitles, QR or landing-page rules, music rights, category restrictions, and whether the account's placement supports the planned CTA.
